# 🚀 Social Advertiser AI — Free Open-Source Social Media Automation Desktop Software

![Social Advertiser Header](https://socialadvertiser.github.io/AG/favicon.ico)

**Social Advertiser** is a powerful, lightweight, and free desktop automation tool designed for marketers, creators, and business owners. Automate your post scheduling, image/video sharing, and cross-platform campaigns across all major social networks directly from your desktop.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Multi-Platform Automation:** Schedule and auto-post content seamlessly across Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, LinkedIn, and Pinterest.
* **AI Content Integration:** Smart scheduling with automated text, custom image/video link management, and custom post intervals (15, 30, 45 mins/hours).
* **Media & Campaign Saver:** Built-in tools to save media links, campaign data, user dropdown settings, and campaign presets effortlessly.
* **Privacy First & Secure:** Runs locally on your machine—no third-party cloud servers store your credentials or personal token data.
* **Lightweight UI:** Modern, responsive, clean light-themed desktop interface for smooth workflow.
